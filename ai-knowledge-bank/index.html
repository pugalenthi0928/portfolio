Source & methodology

A learning system, not a substitute for the books.

Why this exists

This knowledge bank is built from source books and papers, but it does not replace them. The goal is to extract the durable ideas, organise them into questions, and create a learning system that helps readers understand, test, and connect concepts across the AI field.

The point of the bank.

A field as broad as AI cannot be carried in your head as a list. The way to know it is to keep a structured bank of questions you can revisit, test against, and extend as each new book lands. Mastery is a system, not a sprint.
