About

Short version. Long version is in the resume.

I’m a Forward Deployed AI Engineer with a Master of Artificial Intelligence from Monash University. My research focused on foundation models for medical imaging — comparing vision-language and self-supervised encoders across retinal-disease benchmarks with zero-shot and limited-label transfer.

At ZeroFold, I work across technical discovery, solution architecture and delivery for applied AI and software engagements, translating stakeholder requirements into production architectures, APIs, roadmaps and acceptance criteria. Previously, I was a Business Fellow & Campus Ambassador at Perplexity AI, where I designed and tested AI-assisted research workflows across multiple industries.

Based in Melbourne. Open to forward deployed, applied AI and AI solutions engineering roles where I can stay technical, work closely with customers and own problems through delivery.
